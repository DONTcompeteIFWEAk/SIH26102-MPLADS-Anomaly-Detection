IDEA TITLE
❖Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)

• Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

  • Dual-Layer Hybrid Intelligence: Merges a 3-model unsupervised ML ensemble (Isolation Forest 45%, Profile-Deduplicated LOF 35%, PCA 20%) with 5 codified statutory CAG & GFR rules.
  • Real-Time Pre-Sanction Gatekeeper: Screens 100% of public works transactions before fund disbursement, replacing 24-month delayed sample autopsies.
  • National Ingestion Scope: Trained and validated on 105,000 authentic civil works across all 33 States & UTs (2019-2024), monitoring ₹6,260 Cr in scheme capital.

• How it addresses the problem

  • Solves the 5% Audit Blindspot: Traditional CAG audits cover under 8% of transactions after money has left; our system audits 100% of works in <15ms.
  • Stops GFR 149 Evasion: Automatically catches contract-splitting intentionally priced at ₹4.80L–₹4.99L or ₹9.80L–₹9.99L to bypass mandatory GeM e-tenders.
  • Prevents Ghost Duplication: Geospatial NLP detects identical works repeated in the same village/block to block duplicate contractor billing.

• Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

  • 🚨 The "March Rush" Radar: First-of-its-kind temporal forensics detecting Q4 fiscal-dump sanctions before March 31 (3,111 works caught nationwide).
  • ⏳ Chronic Dormancy Engine: Pinpointed ₹287 Cr trapped in 5,960 works stalled >3 years from 2019–2021 for fund clawback and reallocation.
  • 🇮🇳 Bilingual Usability: Instant plain-language legal directives (सरल हिंदी + English) with one-click printable official CAG dossiers.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
• Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
- Data Feasibility: 100% validated on authentic MoSPI datasets (60,359 baseline works expanded to 105,000 multi-year records spanning 2019-2024 across all 33 States/UTs).
- Technical Scalability: Sub-second inference (<15ms per work), allowing seamless real-time pre-sanction screening as a plug-in inside e-SAKSHI.
- Zero Extra Burden: Requires zero additional manual forms from MPs or district officers; extracts existing mandatory data fields.
• Potential challenges and risks
- Algorithmic Risk (Zero-Distance KNN Collapse): Public works have thousands of identical repetitive civil works (handpumps) that distort standard KNN distance calculations.
- Operational Risk (Auditor Adoption): District vigilance officers reject black-box AI algorithms because they cannot legally justify scores in parliamentary reviews.
- False Positive Risk: Legitimate mega-projects (e.g. ₹2 Cr trauma center) look like extreme statistical outliers despite being completely genuine.
• Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Profile-Deduplicated LOF: Groups identical civil works profiles into canonical representations before KNN, preserving true neighborhood reachability density.
- Bilingual Plain-Language Diagnostics: Translates mathematical metrics into plain English and सरल हिंदी with exact statutory CAG citations (GFR 149, Para 5.2).
- Dual-Layer Hybrid Logic & Feedback Loop: Statutory rules validate legitimate high-budget works; human officer resolutions dynamically calibrate decision thresholds.
